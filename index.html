How she feels

When she puts on Beem.

Every choice on this page serves three feelings. If a design, caption or product idea does not create at least one, we leave it out.

Seen

The shades are made to flatter deep, medium and light skin. She finds her color without hunting for it.

Effortless

One swipe, no mirror needed. Beem is for the bus, the office, church, class and the quick stop before the party.

Proud

The tube is pretty enough to leave on the table. Friends ask what she is wearing, and it feels good to say Beem.

We say
  • "Put it on. Go shine."
  • "Five shades. One for every mood."
  • "Looks like you, only glowing."
We avoid
  • "Fix your flaws" or "perfect skin" language
  • Stiff luxury talk that makes her feel outside the brand
  • Pressure, filters and comparison

Step by step

How we build Beem from nothing.

The order matters. Identity first, because everything else (the tube, the box, the website, the photos) is built from it.

  1. Brand foundationsWho she is, what Beem promises her, and five words that describe the brand. This page is a first draft of that.
    Now
  2. Visual identityFinal logo, colors, type, and a one-page brand guide. We build it from your box and your logo file.
    Now
  3. Product and shade briefFinish, scent, texture and the exact look of Clear, Champagne, Shimmer, Ruby and Cocoa Suede on deep, medium and light skin.
    Next
  4. Packaging designTube, outer box, labels and ingredient text, sized to your supplier's real dimensions.
    Next
  5. Make samplesFind a manufacturer, request samples, test on real skin and fix the formula before any large order.
    Later
  6. Register and protectRegister the business with CAC, trademark the name and logo, and start NAFDAC product registration. Check current requirements with each agency.
    Later
  7. Website and paymentsBuild the shop from this design with local payments, delivery zones and a simple checkout.
    Later
  8. Photos, content and launchProduct shots, creators wearing the five shades, a launch date, and an Instagram and TikTok plan.
    Later
