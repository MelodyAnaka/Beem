our story

Beauty should feel easy.

beem is lip gloss for people who want to look and feel lovely without a long routine.

why beem

Soft, simple and yours.

We wanted lip colour that feels as good as it looks: glossy but never sticky, easy to apply and in shades that suit real, everyday life.

So we kept it simple. A squeeze tube for quick touch-ups. A wand gloss for a polished, glassy finish. Warm, flattering shades from soft nude to deep berry.
